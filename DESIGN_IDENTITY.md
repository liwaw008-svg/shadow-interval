# Shadow Interval identity

- Mechanism signature: two-role daylight compatibility review with owner adoption.
- Product metaphor: an outdoor sundial whose translucent shadow is the decision surface.
- Narrative voice: concise spatial designer, never legalistic.
- Visual grammar: off-white open sky, ultraviolet arcs, sulfur sun, ink-blue shadow wedges.
- Interaction grammar: drag two time handles, rotate one orientation dial, then watch the shadow resolve.
- Result presentation: the accepted interval remains cut directly into the clock arc.
- Documentation style: field note from a temporary-space designer.
- Evidence story: direct human-authored records are frozen, hashed, role-attributed, and compared without hosted demo URLs.

Do not use dashboards, stacked cards, document metaphors, raw URL grids, or a repeated stepper.
